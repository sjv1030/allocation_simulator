How this model works

  1. Three asset buckets compound independently, with no rebalancing. All 20 allocations share one set of simulated asset returns.
  2. PL drifts at the guaranteed rate; BEL and MCL grow monthly at their configured annual rates.
  3. At checkpoints, profit or shortfall is measured against drifted PL. Positive profit generates a shareholder profit share. Only the selected fraction of a shortfall is supported.
  4. PSA supplies the covered amount up to its available buffer; Capital injection supplies the remainder. The model permits injections when the covered shortfall exceeds the available PSA buffer; partial coverage does not eliminate injection risk. The PSA floor is calculated from checkpoint PA after the decided profit share, before support. It is not a guaranteed post-settlement PSA/PA ratio.
  5. All capital events land at the start of the following month, proportionally across the existing buckets. PL then resets to max(post-capital PA, queued PV of BEL, queued PV of MCL), before monthly drift.
  6. PV uses a fixed one-year exponent, regardless of checkpoint frequency. Uncovered shortfalls accumulate for reporting only. PSA has no independent investment return in this script.
  7. CAGR and terminal returns exclude capital flows and use exactly the nominal horizon. One additional month exposes the final settlement.

The page defaults to periodic annual checks, matching the PDF and SimConfig. The Python command line defaults to final-only checks unless --periodic_profit_check is passed. Browser seeds are reproducible here, but use a different random generator from NumPy; identical seed numbers do not produce identical Python paths.

Defaults for PSA size, its floor, coverage, BEIR and risk-free rate are assumptions. Initial PA is inferred from referenced materials; the 4.25% guarantee is from an external contact, not directly confirmed by the insurer. The 57% guaranteed-benefit share and 90:10 split are described as insurer-confirmed in the background record.
